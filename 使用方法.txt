1. 这是什么
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

在电脑上给 iPhone / iPad 做「虚拟定位」和「轨迹模拟」的桌面工具。

你可以在地图上画一条路线，加上途经点，然后让手机按真实速度沿着这条路线"跑"起来。
主要用于测试依赖定位的 App（校园跑、打卡、打车、外卖、运动记录等），不用真的出门。

它不是"破解"类工具：走的完全是 Apple 官方的开发者接口（开发者镜像 + DVT 定位服务），
和 Xcode 里做定位调试用的是同一套东西。


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. 安装
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

【Windows】

  方式一（推荐）：下载 iOSLocationTrack-1.1.0_win-setup.exe
      双击安装。安装向导是中文的。

  方式二：下载 iOSLocationTrack-1.1.0_win-portable.zip
      解压到任意目录，双击里面的 iOSLocationTrack.exe 即可，无需安装。

  注意：程序启动时会弹「用户账户控制（UAC）」窗口，需要点「是」。
        这一步是为了让它能自动安装 Apple 设备驱动（见下一节），
        虚拟定位本身并不需要管理员权限。

【macOS】

  下载 iOSLocationTrack-1.1.0_mac.dmg → 打开 → 把「虚拟定位轨迹」拖进「应用程序」。

  首次打开若提示「来自身份不明的开发者」（因为没买 Apple 开发者签名）：
      在应用图标上右键 → 打开 → 再点「打开」。
      只需做一次，之后正常双击即可。


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. 第一次启动
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

【Windows】会先弹出「环境准备」窗口，逐项检查所需的组件：

    ✓ 绿色对勾 = 已就绪，右边按钮显示「已就绪」（不可点）
    ✗ 红色叉号 = 缺失，右边有可点的「安装 / 下载」按钮
    · 灰色圆点 = 可选项，没就绪也不影响使用

  必需项一共 6 个，全部变绿后，右下角的「进入程序」按钮才会亮起：

    1) Python 运行时        程序自带，不用管
    2) pymobiledevice3      程序自带，不用管
    3) Apple Mobile Device Support
         Windows 上用来发现 iPhone 的系统服务。缺失时点「安装」，
         程序会自动下载安装（约 40~200 MB，装完可能提示需要重启电脑）。
    4) RSD 隧道依赖         程序自带，不用管
    5) 开发者镜像           约 15 MB，点「下载」自动获取
    6) 管理员权限           已提权则为绿色

  如果你已经装过 iTunes 或爱思助手，第 3 项通常已经是绿的。

  想跳过这个窗口（依赖都已就绪时）：启动参数加 --no-setup。
  想用文字版检查：在命令行执行  iOSLocationTrack.exe --check-driver

【macOS】没有这个窗口，直接打开界面。


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. 连接 iPhone
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1) 用**数据线**把 iPhone 连到电脑（Wi-Fi 连接不稳定，建议用线）
  2) 解锁手机，屏幕上会弹「要信任此电脑吗？」→ 点「信任」并输入锁屏密码
  3) 手机上打开：设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 开发者模式 → 打开（会要求重启手机）
        * iOS 16 及以上必须开这个，否则连不上
  4) 回到程序界面，点「连接设备」

  连接成功后，界面顶部会显示设备名称和 iOS 版本，状态变成「已连接」。

  耗时参考：实测约 0.8 秒（含隧道建立与开发者会话）。


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. 开始使用
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

基本流程就四步：画路线 → 调参数 → 播放 → 停止。

  第 1 步：画路线
      在地图上依次点击，每点一下加一个点，连成一条线。
      也可以用「导入 GPX」载入已有的轨迹文件，或直接粘贴经纬度坐标。

  第 2 步：加途经点（可选）
      想要路线绕行某处时，把某个点设为途经点，程序会重新规划路径
      （走 OSRM 在线路径规划；离线时退化为直线连接）。

  第 3 步：调参数
      · 速度倍率：1x 约等于正常步行，调大即加速
      · 循环播放：跑到终点后自动回到起点继续
      · 真实感模拟：
           GPS 漂移     —— 让定位有小幅随机晃动，看起来不像"贴线走"
           速度抖动     —— 速度上下浮动，避免匀速被识别
           发送频率     —— 每秒下发几个点，默认 5 Hz

  第 4 步：播放
      点「播放」，手机定位就开始沿路线移动。地图上会显示进度和剩余时间。
      随时可以「暂停」「继续」「拖动进度条跳转」。

  结束：点「停止」
      手机会**立即恢复真实定位**。程序关闭时也会自动恢复，不会把手机"卡"在虚拟位置。

6. 界面功能
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  地图区
    · 点击加点 / 拖拽调整 / 右键删除
    · 滚轮缩放，按住拖拽平移
    · 可切换地图源（高德矢量 / 高德卫星 / Esri 全球卫星 / OSM）
    · 瓦片会缓存到本地，第二次打开同一区域不用重新下载

  路线区
    · 显示总距离、预计耗时、点数
    · 支持 GPX 导入导出

  播放控制
    · 播放 / 暂停 / 停止 / 循环
    · 进度条拖动跳转（seek）
    · 速度倍率 0.5x ~ 10x

  设备区
    · 设备列表与连接状态
    · 「挂载开发者镜像」按钮（一般连接时会自动处理）
    · 「手动 RSD」入口：如果你想用别的工具建的隧道，可以手填地址和端口

  日志区
    · 实时显示程序在做什么，出问题时先看这里

  模拟模式（无需真机）
    · 命令行执行  main.py --mock
    · 界面功能都能用，只是定位不会真的下发到手机，适合先熟悉操作

7. 数据与日志在哪
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Windows
    配置 / 瓦片缓存 / 日志    %LOCALAPPDATA%\iOSLocationTrack\
    程序日志                  %LOCALAPPDATA%\iOSLocationTrack\logs\app.log
    已下载的安装包            %LOCALAPPDATA%\iOSLocationTrack\installers\

  macOS
    配置 / 瓦片缓存 / 日志    ~/Library/Application Support/iOSLocationTrack/

  用记事本打开即可；若用 PowerShell 5.1 的 Get-Content 看，请加 -Encoding UTF8。

8. 常见问题
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q: 设备列表是空的，看不到 iPhone
   依次检查：
     1) 手机是否已解锁，并点了「信任此电脑」
     2) 换一根数据线（很多线只能充电不能传数据）
     3) Windows：环境准备窗口里「Apple Mobile Device Support」是不是绿的？
        不是绿的，点「安装」。已装但服务停了的话，重启一下电脑往往就好了。
     4) 手机上「开发者模式」开了吗？
     5) 关掉爱思助手 / i4Tools / Xcode —— 它们会抢占设备的连接

Q: 点「连接设备」失败，提示隧道建立失败
   通常是同一时间有别的工具占着设备。把所有同类工具关掉再试。
   iOS 17+ 的设备同一时刻只允许一条调试连接。

Q: 播放了，但手机定位没动
     1) 确认手机屏幕上有没有出现定位图标
     2) 看日志区有没有「已下发 lat=..., lon=...」的输出
     3) 确认连的是这台手机（设备列表里有没有多台）
     4) 部分 App 有自己的防作弊逻辑，会忽略系统定位

Q: 提示 "Failed to verify the disk image signature"
   设备拒绝了开发者镜像的签名。本项目面向 **iOS 17 及以上**；
   iOS 16 及更早的系统需要用 Apple 按构建号签发的经典镜像，社区镜像会被拒签。

Q: Windows 上装完驱动提示要重启
   重启电脑再打开程序即可。第一次安装 Apple 驱动时比较常见。

Q: 杀毒软件 / SmartScreen 报警
   因为程序和安装包没有代码签名证书（买证书要花钱）。
   点「更多信息」→「仍要运行」即可。想更保险可以自己从源码构建。

Q: 能同时开两个吗？
   不能。iOS 17+ 的用户态隧道在一个进程里只能有一条，
   而且设备本身也只允许一条调试连接。请只开一个。

Q: 定位会不会"偷偷"恢复成真实的？
   Windows 的用户态隧道偶尔会静默断开，断开后手机会自己回到真实定位。
   程序有保活机制：设了点但没在播放时，每 15 秒重发一次。
   如果发现定位跳回真实位置，点一次「连接设备」重连即可。

Q: 怎么卸载干净？
   Windows：控制面板卸载即可。用户数据在 %LOCALAPPDATA%\iOSLocationTrack，
            不想要可以手动删（卸载程序会保留，方便重装后继续用配置）。
   macOS ：把 App 拖到废纸篓，再删 ~/Library/Application Support/iOSLocationTrack。


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. 已知限制
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  · 只支持 iOS 17 及以上。iOS 16 及更早不支持（原因见上一节）。
  · 只能改经纬度。海拔、定位精度、移动速度这些由 iOS 自己合成，
    无法从外部注入 —— 这是 Apple 接口本身的限制，不是本工具的问题。
  · 需要联网的地方：挂载开发者镜像时（Apple 在线签名）、
    在线路径规划、地图瓦片下载。其余全部在本机完成。
  · 手机必须响应用户操作：解锁、信任、开开发者模式。
  · 一个进程只能有一条隧道，别双开。
  · 不要用于任何违法违规用途。请只在你自己的设备上、用于测试你自己开发的 App。

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. What it is
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A desktop tool for **virtual location** and **route simulation** on iPhone / iPad.

Draw a route on a map, add waypoints, and let the phone "travel" along it at realistic
speed. Built for testing location-dependent apps (running trackers, check-in, ride-hailing,
delivery, fitness logging) without actually going outside.

This is not a jailbreak or a hack: it uses Apple's own developer interfaces (developer
disk image + the DVT location service) — the same mechanism Xcode uses for location
debugging.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. Install
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[Windows]

  Option A (recommended): download iOSLocationTrack-1.1.0_win-setup.exe and run it.

  Option B: download iOSLocationTrack-1.1.0_win-portable.zip, unzip anywhere and
            double-click iOSLocationTrack.exe. No installation needed.

  Note: Windows will show a User Account Control (UAC) prompt on launch — click Yes.
        That is only so the app can auto-install the Apple device driver (see below).
        Virtual location itself does not require administrator rights.

[macOS]

  Download iOSLocationTrack-1.1.0_mac.dmg, open it, and drag the app into Applications.

  If macOS says "unidentified developer" on first launch (the app is not notarized):
      right-click the app icon -> Open -> Open.
      You only need to do this once.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. First launch
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[Windows] An "Environment Check" window appears, listing every required component:

    Green check  = ready; the button on the right reads "already installed" (disabled)
    Red cross    = missing; a clickable Install / Download button appears
    Grey dot     = optional; not required to continue

  Six items are required. The "Enter" button at the bottom-right only becomes
  clickable once all of them are green:

    1) Python runtime          bundled, nothing to do
    2) pymobiledevice3         bundled, nothing to do
    3) Apple Mobile Device Support
         The Windows service that discovers iPhones. If missing, click Install and
         the app downloads it automatically (~40-200 MB; may ask for a reboot).
    4) RSD tunnel dependencies bundled, nothing to do
    5) Developer disk image    ~15 MB, click Download
    6) Administrator rights    green if already elevated

  If you already have iTunes or 3uTools installed, item 3 is usually green already.

  To skip the window when everything is ready, pass --no-setup.
  For a text-mode check, run:  iOSLocationTrack.exe --check-driver

[macOS] There is no such window — the UI opens directly.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. Connect your iPhone
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1) Connect the iPhone with a **USB cable** (Wi-Fi works but is less reliable)
  2) Unlock it. When "Trust This Computer?" appears, tap Trust and enter your passcode
  3) On the phone: Settings -> Privacy & Security -> Developer Mode -> turn on
       (it will ask to restart the phone). Required on iOS 16 and later.
  4) Back in the app, click "Connect device"

  When it succeeds, the device name and iOS version appear at the top of the window
  and the status becomes "connected".

  Measured time: about 0.8 seconds including tunnel setup and the developer session.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. Basic usage
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Four steps: draw -> tune -> play -> stop.

  Step 1: Draw a route
      Click on the map to add points one by one. You can also import a GPX file,
      or paste latitude/longitude coordinates directly.

  Step 2: Add waypoints (optional)
      Mark a point as a waypoint to force the route through it; the app re-plans
      the path (via OSRM online routing, falling back to straight lines offline).

  Step 3: Tune the parameters
      - Speed multiplier  1x is roughly walking pace; increase to go faster
      - Loop              restart from the beginning after reaching the end
      - Realism model
          GPS drift       small random wander, so movement doesn't look pinned to the line
          Speed jitter    fluctuates the speed so it isn't suspiciously constant
          Send rate       how many points per second (default 5 Hz)

  Step 4: Play
      Click Play and the phone's location starts moving along the route. The map shows
      progress and remaining time. You can Pause / Resume / drag the seek bar at any time.

  Finishing: click Stop
      The phone **immediately returns to its real location**. Closing the app also
      restores it — the phone will never be left stuck at a fake position.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. Feature reference
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Map
    - Click to add points, drag to move, right-click to delete
    - Scroll to zoom, drag to pan
    - Switchable tile sources (AMap vector / AMap satellite / Esri imagery / OSM)
    - Tiles are cached locally, so revisiting an area needs no re-download

  Route
    - Shows total distance, estimated duration and point count
    - GPX import / export

  Playback
    - Play / Pause / Stop / Loop
    - Seek by dragging the progress bar
    - Speed multiplier 0.5x - 10x

  Device
    - Device list and connection state
    - "Mount developer image" button (normally handled automatically on connect)
    - "Manual RSD" entry: if you built a tunnel with another tool, enter host and port

  Log
    - Shows what the app is doing in real time — check this first when something fails

  Mock mode (no device needed)
    - Run  main.py --mock  from the command line
    - The whole UI works; locations just aren't sent to a phone. Good for a first look.

8. Troubleshooting
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q: The device list is empty — no iPhone visible
   Check in order:
     1) Is the phone unlocked, and did you tap "Trust This Computer"?
     2) Try another cable (many cables are charge-only, no data lines)
     3) Windows: is "Apple Mobile Device Support" green in the Environment Check?
        If not, click Install. If it is installed but the service is stopped,
        a reboot usually fixes it.
     4) Is Developer Mode enabled on the phone?
     5) Close 3uTools / i4Tools / Xcode — they compete for the same device connection

Q: "Connect device" fails with a tunnel error
   Usually another tool is holding the device. Close every similar tool and retry.
   An iOS 17+ device only allows one debugging connection at a time.

Q: It is playing but the phone's location doesn't move
     1) Does the location arrow appear on the phone?
     2) Does the log show "sent lat=..., lon=..." lines?
     3) Are you connected to the right phone (is more than one listed)?
     4) Some apps have their own anti-spoofing and ignore the system location

Q: "Failed to verify the disk image signature"
   The device rejected the developer image's signature. This project targets
   **iOS 17 and later**; iOS 16 and earlier need classic images signed by Apple
   per build number, and community images are rejected.

Q: Windows asks for a reboot after installing the driver
   Reboot and start the app again. This is common on the first Apple driver install.

Q: Antivirus / SmartScreen warnings
   The app and installer are not code-signed (certificates cost money).
   Click "More info" -> "Run anyway". If you prefer, build it yourself from source.

Q: Can I run two instances?
   No. iOS 17+ allows only one userspace tunnel per process, and the device itself
   permits only one debugging connection. Run a single instance.

Q: Does the location silently revert to the real one?
   The Windows userspace tunnel can occasionally drop, after which the phone returns
   to its real location by itself. The app has a keepalive: when a location is set but
   playback is stopped, it re-sends every 15 seconds. If you notice a revert, just
   click "Connect device" again.

Q: How do I uninstall cleanly?
   Windows: uninstall from Control Panel. User data lives in
            %LOCALAPPDATA%\iOSLocationTrack and is intentionally kept, so a reinstall
            retains your config — delete the folder manually if you want it gone.
   macOS:   drag the app to the Trash, then delete
            ~/Library/Application Support/iOSLocationTrack.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. Known limitations
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - iOS 17 and later only. iOS 16 and earlier are not supported (see above).
  - Latitude and longitude only. Altitude, horizontal accuracy and speed are synthesized
    by iOS and cannot be injected from outside — that is a limitation of Apple's
    interface, not of this tool.
  - Network is needed for: mounting the developer image (Apple signs it online),
    online route planning, and map tile downloads. Everything else runs locally.
  - The phone must cooperate: unlock it, trust the computer, enable Developer Mode.
  - One tunnel per process — do not run two instances.
  - Do not use this for anything illegal. Use it only on your own devices, to test
    apps you are developing.
